### Post 3: The 60-30-10 Rule
The Golden Ratio for UI Colors:
- 60% Neutral (Backgrounds)
- 30% Secondary (Cards/Sidebars)
- 10% Accent (CTAs)

Stop the "color noise" and guide your users. 🚀
