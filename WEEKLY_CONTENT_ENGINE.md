# WEEKLY CONTENT ENGINE: PALETTEFLOW EDITION

This document outlines the repeatable weekly content strategy for PaletteFlow to build massive semantic surface area and dominate the color palette generator niche.

---

## 1. THE REPEATABLE WEEKLY TEMPLATE SYSTEM

### Template A – Cluster Article (700-1000 words)
*Targeting educational depth and long-tail keywords.*

- **H1:** `[PRIMARY_KEYWORD]`: Complete Guide for `[TARGET_AUDIENCE]` in 2026
- **AI Snapshot:** 30-word intro definition (Direct answer for Google AI Overview).
- **H2:** Why `[PRIMARY_KEYWORD]` Matters for `[INDUSTRY/USE_CASE]`
- **H2:** How to `[ACTION]` with `[PRIMARY_KEYWORD]` (Step-by-step guide).
- **H2:** Top `[NUMBER]` `[TOOLS/STRATEGIES]` for `[PRIMARY_KEYWORD]`
- **H3:** Detailed breakdown of each (with real HEX code examples).
- **H2:** Common Mistakes to Avoid (Include a comparison table).
- **AEO Box:** 5 Key Takeaways (Bulleted list for snippets).
- **AEO Box:** FAQ Section (5 questions + **FAQPage Schema**).
- **Internal Links:** 1 Pillar Page + 2 Cluster Articles + Generator Tool.
- **Example:** "Color Psychology for SaaS Founders: The 2026 Guide"

### Template B – Programmatic Comparison Page (300-500 words)
*Targeting "VS" keywords and competitor search traffic.*

- **H1:** `[ENTITY_A]` vs `[ENTITY_B]`: Which Color Tool is Better for `[USE_CASE]`?
- **Quick Verdict:** 1-sentence AI summary (Bolded for snippets).
- **Comparison Table:** (Features | `[ENTITY_A]` | `[ENTITY_B]` | Winner).
- **H2:** Deep Dive: `[ENTITY_A]` (Pros/Cons + Unique Features).
- **H2:** Deep Dive: `[ENTITY_B]` (Pros/Cons + Unique Features).
- **H2:** When to Choose `[ENTITY_A]` vs `[ENTITY_B]`.
- **Schema:** **Product Comparison Schema** (if applicable) or **Review Schema**.
- **Internal Links:** Pillar Page + PaletteFlow Generator.
- **Example:** "PaletteFlow vs Coolors: Which is better for UI Designers?"

### Template C – Micro-Answer Page (300-500 words)
*Targeting "What is" questions and featured snippets.*

- **H1:** What is `[COLOR_TERM]`? (Direct question format).
- **AI Snapshot:** 40-60 word precise definition.
- **H2:** How Does `[TERM]` Work in Modern Design?
- **H2:** Real-World Example of `[TERM]` in `[INDUSTRY]`.
- **H2:** Why is `[TERM]` Important for Accessibility?
- **H2:** `[TERM]` vs `[RELATED_TERM]` (Side-by-side comparison table).
- **AEO Box:** Core Principles (3-5 bullets).
- **Schema:** **FAQPage Schema** (for the single question).
- **Internal Links:** 1 Cluster Article + Generator Tool.
- **Example:** "What is WCAG Contrast Ratio?"

### Template D – Pillar Page (3000-7000 words)
*The high-authority hub for a broad topic cluster.*

- **H1:** The Ultimate 2026 Guide to `[BROAD_TOPIC]`
- **AI Snapshot:** Executive Summary (200 words for AI Overviews).
- **Navigation:** Sticky Table of Contents with jump links.
- **Chapters:**
    1. Introduction & Evolution of `[TOPIC]`.
    2. Technical Foundations (The "How-To").
    3. Top 10 Advanced Strategies.
    4. Real-World Case Study (e.g., Brand Reimagined).
    5. Future Trends & Predictions for 2027.
- **AEO Box:** Statistical Data Table (Proprietary or aggregated data).
- **AEO Box:** 10+ FAQ with **FAQPage Schema**.
- **Schema:** **Article Schema** + **BreadcrumbList**.
- **Internal Links:** 15+ Cluster Articles + All Tool Pages.
- **Example:** "The Ultimate Guide to Modern Color Theory for Web Developers"

### Template E – Industry Use-Case Page (600-800 words)
*Targeting commercial intent and specific professional niches.*

- **H1:** The Best Color Palettes for `[INDUSTRY]` Brands in 2026
- **Introduction:** Why color choices are critical for `[INDUSTRY]` conversion.
- **H2:** 5 Curated `[INDUSTRY]` Palettes (Visual grid with HEX codes).
- **H2:** Why These Palettes Work (Psychology & Harmony breakdown).
- **H2:** How to Apply These to Your `[INDUSTRY]` Website.
- **AEO Box:** Quick Reference Table (Industry → Best Harmony → HEX Codes).
- **Schema:** **ItemList Schema** (for the palettes).
- **Internal Links:** Pillar Page + Industry Tool + Generator.
- **Example:** "The Best Color Palettes for Luxury Real Estate in 2026"

### Template F – Data/Statistics Article (800-1200 words)
*Targeting backlinks and authority citations.*

- **H1:** 2026 `[COLOR/INDUSTRY]` Design Statistics: The Data You Need
- **The Bottom Line:** 3-sentence AI citation.
- **H2:** Top 10 `[TOPIC]` Statistics (Bulleted list).
- **H2:** Year-over-Year Growth in `[TREND]` (Comparison table).
- **H2:** Deep Dive: Why `[STATISTIC]` is Changing Design.
- **Methodology:** How the data was collected.
- **AEO Box:** Visual Data Summary Table.
- **Schema:** **Dataset Schema** (if applicable) or **Article Schema**.
- **Internal Links:** Pillar Page + Relevant Clusters.
- **Example:** "2026 E-commerce Color Conversion Statistics"
